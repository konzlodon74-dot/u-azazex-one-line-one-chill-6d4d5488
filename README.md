# One Line, One Chill

A single sentence of horror, under 200 characters, written to frighten a reader in one breath. A hospital nurse leans in with a whisper that quietly turns a familiar, comforting nightly ritual into something dreadful: the man who had been tucking the patient in every night has been dead for three days. The scare is earned by what it implies rather than announced, and the final word lands the blow.
